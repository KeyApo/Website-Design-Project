About the studio

Hi, I'm Keymy, or Neon as the Internet has come to know me.

NeonReflections is my corner of the internet for those like me who just enjoy creating. For many years, I have been told that all I make is nerd stuff, but I do have to agree. I am a nerd and I an unapologetically myself having to make things that I personally find joy in. I want that joy to rub off on others, whether they see the effort I put into my characters, fanworks, or merch. I want others to have a little piece of what I want to bring to the world and feel inspired to do the same for others.

I hope you all will see the love I put into each and every artwork I create.

Thanks for visiting and following the work as it grows.
